Key Equations
Schrödinger Equation
$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2\psi}{dx^2}+V(x)\,\psi=E\psi$$
Eigenvalue equation for stationary states. ψ(x) is the wavefunction, E the energy eigenvalue.
Numerov Recurrence
$$\psi_{n+1}=\frac{2\psi_n\!\left(1-\tfrac{5}{12}h^2k_n^2\right)-\psi_{n-1}\!\left(1+\tfrac{1}{12}h^2k_{n-1}^2\right)}{1+\tfrac{1}{12}h^2k_{n+1}^2}$$
$k_n^2=\tfrac{2m}{\hbar^2}[E-V(x_n)]$, step $h=0.025$ a.u., 6th-order accurate.
Potentials Used
Step: $V=\dfrac{V_0}{1+e^{x/T}}$
Well: $V_s(-x{-}a)+V_s(x{-}a)$
Harmonic: $V=\tfrac{1}{2}m\omega^2x^2$
Bound State Condition
$$\psi(x)\to 0\quad\text{as}\quad|x|\to\infty$$
A physical eigenstate must be square-integrable. The ψ-tail value near zero indicates a true bound state.
