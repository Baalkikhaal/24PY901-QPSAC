# Quantum Principles, Structures and Computing

Welcome to the Quantum Principles, Structures and Computing course! This course is designed to introduce students to the fundamental principles that govern quantum mechanics, the structures utilized in quantum computing, and the vast potential of quantum systems for computation.

## Course Overview

In this course, we will explore:
- Basic concepts of quantum mechanics
- Quantum bits and quantum gates
- Quantum algorithms and their applications
- Theoretical and practical implications of quantum computing

Stay tuned for an exciting journey into the world of quantum science!
